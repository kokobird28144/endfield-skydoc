4.6 Equivalence between Adaptive Operation and Solving Linear Systems ​

Source page ID: wS5pCdiNNL0UXUvUi1A1Ak ｜ Open in Tencent Docs

Feed inputs into an adaptive line, and it automatically balances them into quantities of products...

—Does that mean we can make a production line solve linear equations for us?!?!

So I asked AI thought about how this works: it is an iterative solution converging to a result. Suppose

a1x+b1y=c1a2x+b2y=c2

Guess initial x1,y1, then iterate. When the coefficient determinant is greater than 0, a1b2-a2b1>0, use the first equation for next x and the second for next y:

a1xn+1+b1yn=c1a2xn+b2yn+1=c2

The iteration is

xn+1=(c1−b1yn)/a1yn+1=(c2−a2xn)/b2

This converges recursively. Its spectral radius is (absolute values were originally present, but removed because coefficients are positive):

ρ=a2b1a1b2

The positive determinant means spectral radius below 1, hence convergence, with error reduced to ρ times its former value per iteration. Larger a1b2 gives faster convergence. In a line, fewer cross-components mean faster balancing (perhaps).
