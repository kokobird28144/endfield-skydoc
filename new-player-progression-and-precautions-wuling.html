New-Player Progression and Precautions: Wuling ​

Source page ID: L0hsh88XqUwFigit3JjUTg | Open in Tencent Docs

Before reading: players rushing progression and players taking their time have vastly different experiences in Wuling. Rushing requires very tight scheduling and resource allocation; relaxed play has little pressure and will not be discussed in detail. This guide helps players rushing progression plan their route and use resources sensibly.

Stage Zero: What to Know Before Entering Wuling ​

1.After creating an account and entering the game, follow the fixed opening story and tutorial. Once events unlock, accept the special “Main Story Early Unlock: Wuling” event mission to enter Wuling directly.

2.Wuling's main-story route is Jingyu Valley → Wuling City → Qingbo Stockade → Marker Stone → Yinglung Pass → North Wuling Exclusion Zone → Si's Painting. The other areas are side-story maps.

3.Every blueprint in this guide needs water, Precipitation Acid, Inergen and Xiragen connected. The connections are marked with Control Ports in obvious locations. Unless specifically stated otherwise, each Control Port corresponds to one water/Precipitation Acid pump. The blueprints include many safeguards; modifying them on your own is not recommended.

4.Collect as many survey posts as possible while doing missions to accumulate Industry Points. If points are tight, unlock exactly what each stage's diagram shows. Otherwise, even after placing a blueprint you may be unable to use it, and some facilities' output settings may even be disrupted!

5.Stock Bills are extremely tight early on. Spend every bit where it counts—on the blade's edge, not its back, handle or sheath! The upgrade/unlock priorities are shown below: red first, yellow next, then green. Upgrade the rest gradually from cheapest to most expensive.

Priority for area unlocks/upgrades and facility unlocks/upgrades

6.The diagram shows mission progress, construction level, mineral output and precautions at each milestone for players rushing progression. Every blueprint here matches the minimum mineral output at its stage, so mineral demand will not exceed supply.

7.Note that this guide uses 11 blueprints in total. Every stage uses embedded incremental blueprints that draw from or fill gaps in the previous stage's line. Ensure the previous stage's blueprints are complete and unmodified! Otherwise, later blueprints may fail to be placed, or may be placed but fail to run.

8.This is a base-building guide; content outside base building is not covered unless necessary.

9.Skipping Valley IV and entering Wuling directly unlocks Valley IV technology and gives 50 of each facility it unlocks. However, you will still lack enough Depot Unloaders, and the Amethyst Ore needed to craft them is available only in Valley IV. Although Valley IV's main-story tutorial asks you to make Amethyst Parts, its earlier steps have you place a Portable Mining Rig on Originium Ore south of the Hub base, manually collect the ore, put it into a powered Refining Unit, and submit the resulting Origocrust. New players may copy that approach: place a Portable Mining Rig on Amethyst Ore west of the base → manually collect Amethyst Ore → put it in a Refining Unit for Amethyst Fiber → manually move that fiber into a Fitting Unit for Amethyst Parts… So, by Stage Five at the latest, set up an Amethyst Parts line in Valley IV to produce the essential facilities!

10.As of October 30, 2026, these blueprints have undergone over 1 month of development and testing, with no problems so far. If you encounter issues, discuss them in the group chat; whenever I am around, I will answer.

Stage One: Finish Wuling City's Main Story (Unlock Wuling Base and Sky King Flats Outpost) ​

Connect all mining spots in Jingyu Valley and Wuling City, reaching 250/min Originium Ore and 60/min Ferrium Ore, with construction level 2. Pick up the required plants around the base; only 1 of each is needed. Unlock the 5 items shown in the Industry Points diagram. A mission unlocks the environmental monitoring station without spending Stock Bills and gives a photography assignment: your first payout of 12500 Stock Bills! Use it to unlock Wuling City Base's depot bus. Place the first blueprint there and connect its Control Ports to two water pumps for the initial startup.

Stage Two: Finish Marker Stone's Main Story (Unlock Marker Stone Outpost) ​

Fully connect the mining spots, remembering the new spots that appear in Wuling City and Jingyu Valley. Reach 360/min Originium Ore, 90/min Ferrium Ore and 120/min Cuprium Ore, with construction level 5. Unlock the 4 items shown in the Industry Points diagram. Expand Wuling Base's area once and add one Depot Bus Section. Ways to obtain the required Stock Bills, from easiest to hardest:

  1. Unlocking an outpost immediately gives it a Stock Bill reserve at half its cap. Exchange batteries/medicine to collect it.
  2. Besides the environmental monitoring assignment provided by the main story, Wuling has another worth 54000 Stock Bills. Take a photo at the specified location.
  3. The storage node at Wuling's lower right costs 12500 Stock Bills to unlock and offers 3 orders worth 11w+ Stock Bills each (w stands for ten thousand). This is time-consuming and laborious, so it is not recommended.

Stage Three: Finish the North Wuling Exclusion Zone's Main Story (Unlock Yinglung Pass Outpost) ​

Connect every mining spot and gas vent: 360/min Originium Ore, 90/min Ferrium Ore, 220/min Cuprium Ore and 30/min Xiragen, with construction level 7. Unlock every Industry Point except the turrets and the 12 Forges of the Sky unlocked by the “Bring Back the Spring” quest (provisional name). Unlock the depot bus at both Marker Stone and Yinglung Pass.

Stage Four: Complete the Side-Story Maps (Cangjian Valley, Experimental Park, Cedar Forest, and Gapfill Outpost in the North Wuling Exclusion Zone) and Unlock Some Facilities ​

Unlock or upgrade facilities costing 10w Stock Bills or less (w = ten thousand), and connect every mining spot and gas vent: 500/min Originium Ore, 90/min Ferrium Ore, 330/min Cuprium Ore and 120/min Xiragen, with construction level 10. Unlock all Industry Points except the turrets. Expand Wuling Base once to its maximum 80*80 area. Add one depot bus section at Marker Stone and expand it to maximum area. Expand Yinglung Pass once (if you already expanded it for the previous stage's supplementary blueprint, do not expand it again!)

If, after unlocking the areas, you lack Stock Bills for facility unlocks/upgrades, your supply at this point is 380/min Originium Ore, 90/min Ferrium Ore, 310/min Cuprium Ore and 120/min Xiragen, with construction level 7. You can defer the Yinglung Pass blueprint until you reach the required mineral output.

Stage Five: Unlock and Upgrade Almost All Facilities, with Almost All Outposts at Level 4 ​

Warning! This stage requires a production line in Valley IV! See Stage Zero, point 9.

Unlock and upgrade almost all facilities, bring almost every outpost to level 4, and connect every mining spot and gas vent: 510/min Originium Ore, 120/min Ferrium Ore, 540/min Cuprium Ore and 150/min Xiragen. Add one Depot Bus Section at Wuling City.
