Key Mechanism: Where High-Quality Ingredients Come From ​

Dishes use both _1 and _2 grades (Cabbage/Cabbage [A], Meat/Meat [A], Egg/Egg [A], etc.).
_2 is not grown or raised separately; it is an additional output from the same harvest:

Crops (PlantingDataTable → RewardTable)

PlotOutput
soil_corp_cabbage_1Cabbage×30
soil_corp_cabbage_2Cabbage×30, Cabbage [A]×30
soil_sp_4Amber Rice×6
soil_sp_fast_4Amber Rice×30

  • soil_corp_xxx_1: produces only basic crops (those marked enableMutation can also mutate)

  • soil_corp_xxx_2: simultaneously produces basic ×30 and high-quality ×30

  • soil_sp_fast_4: yields 30 per harvest (ordinary soil_sp_4 yields only 6); this is a fast-growing plot
