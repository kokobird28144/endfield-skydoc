Why stress connecting a single-port conduit to a manifold? A manifold-to-manifold connection allows 2–4/s liquid/gas transport, whereas the “conduit manifold–Pipe Converger” priority structure sends the portion above 2/s into the lower-priority pipe. Its priority is therefore incomplete. For example, suppose a manifold-to-manifold connection carries “1/s for 1s, 2/s for 1s, 3/s for 1s.” The average flow is 2/s, so ideally nothing should enter the lower-priority pipe of that structure. In practice, during “3/s for 1s,” 2/s goes into the priority pipe and 1/s into the lower-priority pipe for that 1s. The lower-priority pipe unexpectedly receives an average of 1/3/s! Connecting a single-port conduit to a manifold directly caps the rate at 2/s, making this priority structure complete.

Frequently Asked Questions ​

Most problems can be fixed by replacing the production lines. For a more precise approach, see “Warm-Up” below.

!!! Do not place the next blueprint while facilities from the previous blueprint are unfinished yellow construction ghosts. Craft and submit the facilities first, or the priority structure will break!!!

!!! Do not make changes inside these production lines beyond those demonstrated in the video!!!

When posting a production report in the group or bilibili comments for troubleshooting, always show these items. You can star items in the report to pin them for easy viewing:
Originium Ore, Ferrium Ore, Cuprium Ore, Cuprium, Ferrium Powder, Cuprium Powder, Dense Originium Powder, Hetonite, Xiranite, Heavy Xiranite, Xircon, Pyrrolite, Separator Core, Cuprium Solution, Cuprium Gas, Hetonite Gas, Pyrrolite Gas, SC Wuling Battery (default ordering, ascending).

If you do not follow this, we may well ignore your question.

  1. The Jingyu Valley blueprint will not fit: watch the tutorial carefully and move the conduits as demonstrated. Move each Conduit Inlet entirely outside the base boundary. Check for dropped items inside the base (rare).

  2. Yinglung Pass hits the pipe-component limit: replace the acid conduit with a manifold and remove its Pipe Converger; do the same for the Inergen vents. Check whether you placed many pipe components as position markers at the essence-farming location.

  3. Fluctuations in the production report are normal: adaptive lines oscillate extensively to control reaction rates automatically. Do not panic. Machines switching on and off are normal too; this oscillating adaptive input supply avoids wasting any Xiranite, which is why our output is so high. For the device details, see Some Simpler Adaptive Setups.

  4. Consumption exceeding production for Xiranite, Ferrium, Cuprium, and Dense Originium Powder is normal. In fact, from the time Xiranite/Cuprium still has stored reserves until the final steady state—Xiranite/Cuprium storage empty and Separator Core/Xircon storage full—the line provides the optimal Pyrrolite/Heavy Xiranite ratio for “total Xiranite/Cuprium produced during this period + Xiranite/Cuprium initially in storage.” In other words, provided storage is not full for everything, a warmed-up line is actually operating in its complete form all along.

  5. Since, as above, Xiranite consumption exceeds production, its storage will eventually reach zero before any product storage fills. Is there a way to retain some Xiranite? See “Warm-Up” below: 1.5 Automatic Maximum Gross Production.

  6. If generation is no more than 9800 but you still suffer a major outage, with Cuprium storage full, the decoupling module has a problem. Most likely you lacked 500+ Jincao when placing the blueprint. Prepare enough Jincao and replace the main-base blueprints.

  7. Pyrrolite production around 60/min most likely means Hetonite storage is not full. Our automatic switching produces Hetonite and Pyrrolite at 1:2 while Hetonite storage is not full. Leave it alone: Pyrrolite, Heavy Xiranite, and batteries easily redeem all vouchers. If you really do not want Hetonite, close the Hetonite Gas control port beside the crucible by enabling its passing-quantity limit at 1.

  8. If Hetonite/Pyrrolite production drops sharply—halved or worse, though a smaller drop can also have this cause—check upstream of the North Wuling Exclusion Zone's Xiragen Conduit Outlet Manifold, namely the inlet connected to it. If it is a Conduit Inlet Manifold, use only one of its ports; keep the other free of Xiragen and merge Xiragen into just one port. Alternatively, replace it with a single-port Conduit Inlet. Another possibility: you simply have not connected 80 Xiragen from deposits. One high-purity gas vent supplies 20 and one low-purity deposit 10; do not connect too few. Effects on Existing Lines ​

Too many additional unloaders may starve the Xircon line of Xiranite, and the Separator Core line of Xiranite/Cuprium, with undesirable results. Check whether stored Separator Core and Xircon quantities decline. If neither declines, there is no problem.
