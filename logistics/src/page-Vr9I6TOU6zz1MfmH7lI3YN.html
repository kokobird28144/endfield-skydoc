Tutorial: Floating Devices in Farming Areas ​

Source page ID: Vr9I6TOU6zz1MfmH7lI3YN | Open in Tencent Docs

Contributor: 卖兔子的小智乃

Last updated: 2026/10/20

Video: https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1FkJB6oEuq

Foreword ​

At present, this tutorial's device-floating technique works only in the farming areas in Wuling City and Si's Painting. Because of the height limit for floating devices, it can be used only to overlap fields and devices; it cannot overlap one device with another.

Because of the height-detection issue, a floating sprinkler cannot detect fields, so sprinklers cannot be floated.

Purpose ​

Floating devices allows upgraded fields to receive a higher adjacent-crop bonus. The best field layout is given in Research and Application of Farming and Automatic Harvester Mechanics.

Method ​

Devices and pipe parts with pipe entrances or exits (pipe splitters, pipe mergers, and pipe bridges) can be floated. There are two methods. Fields can be placed before floating a device; this does not affect the process and makes it easier to see whether the device has floated successfully.

Method 1 (the method used in the video) ​

Step 1: Choose the intended device position. Place a pipe part at a nearby higher position and draw a pipe starting from it.

Step 2: Extend the pipe to a point three grid spaces beyond the intended device port, then place the pipe. (On PC, press R to change the pipe's priority direction.)

Step 3: Delete the three pipe segments at the end.

Step 4: Align the device's pipe port with the pipe and place the device. (If the device has only a pipe exit, reverse the pipe flow direction first.)

Method 2 (most stable; Method 1 must be used once first) ​

Tests show that a device can be floated by connecting it to a device or pipe part that is already floating, which gives us the second method. This method is the most stable and can reliably float devices in places where Method 1 is less reliable, but it requires a floating device or pipe part produced by Method 1. Before using Method 2, it is best to place the fields first so that the pipe parts are not placed directly on the ground.

Step 1: Choose the intended device position. Draw a pipe from the pipe exit of an already-floating device or pipe part to the intended position. (If there is only a pipe entrance, first place a pipe splitter against the entrance, then draw the pipe from the splitter's exit.)

Step 2: Place pipe parts along the pipe.

Step 3: Align the device's pipe port with the pipe part and place the device.
