If you can't solve a problem, identify the person taking over, tell the requester clearly, and hand it over. Avoid taking frustrations out on anyone or quitting partway through. An unexplained abandonment earns a warning, and another such incident soon afterward costs the helper their moderator role.

Members may invite a bot into the chat after getting permission from at least two moderators. Keep it under close supervision. For an unauthorized invitation, warn the member and remove the bot. Remove both on a repeat offense. If a bot disrupts troubleshooting, handle it as you would a disruptive member. Afterward, the support team can discuss whether to penalize the owner (the moderators did approve adding it, after all).

A repeat offense usually occurs within two to three days. Escalate penalties across different rules except where a first-offense warning applies. For example, after a two-hour mute for two personal attacks, disrupting troubleshooting would earn a one-day mute rather than another ten-minute mute. Conversely, someone muted for an hour for disruption who then adds a bot without permission should receive the first-offense warning and only be removed if they repeat that bot violation.

P.S. Depending on the behavior and its severity, group moderators may require a name change, mute a member, or remove them. Discuss borderline cases with the other moderators.
