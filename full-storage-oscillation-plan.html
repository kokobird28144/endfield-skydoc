How This Build Improves on the Last
The 1.5 factory achieved automatic balancing by keeping Xiranite and Cuprium stock at 0. That had drawbacks.
Players found that having 0 Xiranite in storage made it inconvenient to supply quests and craft power poles.
With 0 Xiranite stock, withdrawal-port behavior also occasionally broke automatic balancing. Combined with other handling errors, this could break the Xircon line.

If you do not follow this, we may well ignore your question.

  1. The line reaches maximum steady-state output once storage is full for Xiranite, Cuprium, Ferrium Powder, Separator Cores, Xircon, Hetonite Parts, and Hetonite. Output can exceed that steady rate while Cuprium Powder, Dense Originium Powder, Pyrrolite, and Ferrium are still in stock. That is expected.

  1. If output drops below 360 after Xiranite storage fills, deposit priority at the Wuling City Liquid Xiranite oscillation module may have broken. See the repair section at the end.

Discretionary Withdrawals (Draft) ​

Withdrawal ports can draw Cuprium, Xiranite, Heavy Xiranite, Hetonite, or Pyrrolite.

Too many withdrawal ports can starve the Xircon line of Xiranite or the Separator Core line of Xiranite or Cuprium. Watch whether stocks of Separator Cores or Xircon decline. If they hold steady, the withdrawals are fine.

If you're unsure, place at most 2 withdrawal ports per item type.
